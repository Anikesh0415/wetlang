Wetworld: First-Principles Organoid Intelligence Architecture

Wetworld is an autonomous, open-source biocomputing architecture designed to pioneer self-sustaining biological AI using human neural organoids coupled to neuromorphic silicon at <20W edge power. Authored and architected by Anikesh Tiwari.

1. The Metabolic Perfusion Boundary (Krogh Cylinder Diffusion)

Oxygen diffusion in 3D neural tissue follows Fick’s Second Law: ∇²P_O2 = R_metabolic / (D · α).
Without active microfluidic replenishment, tissue beyond critical radius r_crit ≈ 200–300 µm undergoes anoxic necrosis. Wetworld’s WW-16 manifold maintains laminar flow (Re ≈ 1.2 in feed channels) transitioning to creeping Stokes flow (Re < 0.1) in the culture well, maintaining shear stress strictly at τ ≈ 0.05 dyn/cm² (well below the 0.1–0.5 dyn/cm² cell damage limit).

2. Low-Noise Biopotential Acquisition (Tier 2 AFE)

Extracellular action potentials range between 20–100 µV. Immersed in saline electrolyte, massive electrochemical DC half-cell potentials (±500 mV) overwhelm standard amplifiers. Wetworld implements Capacitive-Coupled Instrumentation Amplifiers (CCIA) with sub-threshold pseudo-resistors (R_eq ≥ 1.0–1.6 TΩ) and a solid L2 ground plane to enforce input-referred noise < 2.0 µVrms.

3. Spike-Timing-Dependent Plasticity (STDP)

Synaptic weights rewire dynamically according to millisecond spike timing differences: Δw = A₊ exp(-Δt / τ₊) for pre-before-post firing (LTP), and Δw = -A₋ exp(Δt / τ₋) for post-before-pre firing (LTD). To induce learning, the closed-loop round-trip latency must strictly remain < 10 ms.

4. The Free Energy Principle (FEP) in Biological Computing

Living neural networks have no backpropagation. Following Karl Friston’s Free Energy Principle, biological circuits act to minimize thermodynamic and variational entropy (surprise). When embodied in an environment (e.g. Inverted Pendulum), delivering predictable, structured sensory pulses during correct behavior and unpredictable chaotic bursts upon error compels the organoid to physically reorganize its synaptic connections to stabilize the system.
